About us

Ghana's gist, told right

Fresh music, movies, football and culture from Ghana and beyond, with a community built around it.

Last updated 3 November 2026

Hottest Gist GH is a home for Ghana's music, entertainment and culture, built for the fans who live it. Every day we bring you the songs climbing the charts, the shows worth watching, the Black Stars and local football, and the stories everyone is talking about, in clear, short reads you can trust and share.

We started in 2026 with a simple belief: Ghana's creative scene deserves a place that is fast, fair and fun, where news is told straight, artists get a real platform, and fans are part of the conversation instead of just watching it.

Music firstNew releases, chart moves, concerts, awards and the artists to watch, from Afrobeats and highlife to hiplife, drill and gospel.
Entertainment and trendsMovies, TV, celebrities and what is buzzing on TikTok, Instagram, X and Facebook in Ghana.
SportsThe Black Stars, the Ghana Premier League and Ghanaians making headlines abroad.

“Tell it straight, credit the source, and give the next big Ghanaian artist a stage.”

Our standards

  • We credit our sources. When a story comes from another outlet, we name it and link to it, and we write in our own words.
  • Facts before buzz. News about releases, charts, concerts, awards and sport goes up quickly. Gossip and rumours are checked by a person before anything is published, or left out.
  • We fix mistakes. If we get something wrong, tell us at our WhatsApp channel and we will correct it and say so.
  • Respect for creators. Names, music, photos and logos belong to their owners. If you own something shown here and want it changed or removed, we act on it promptly.

More than a news site

Create a free account and Hottest Gist GH becomes your community. Like and react to stories, comment, vote in polls and in the weekly Ghana Top 10, test yourself in the quiz, sign the fan wall, add friends and climb the Top fans board. Fans can post their own gist, photos and videos, build a following and earn the gold tick.

For artists

Every week we listen to new music from across Ghana. Send your song through Submit your song, and the best get a story on the site and a place in New Releases. Artists and managers can also book the Featured artist spotlight at the top of our homepage.

For brands and creators

Reach a young, engaged Ghanaian audience through our sponsor slot, featured listings on the Services board, or partnerships around releases and events. Creators can list their services, and brands can find the right talent.

Talk to us

News tips, corrections, takedown requests, adverts and partnerships: reach us through our WhatsApp channel. Follow our WhatsApp channel to get the gist first.
