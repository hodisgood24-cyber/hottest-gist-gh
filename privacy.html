01Who we are

Hottest Gist GH is a Ghanaian music, entertainment and news website. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data with care and in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

In short: we only collect what we need to run the site, we never sell your data, and you can ask us to see or delete it at any time.

02What we collect

When you create an account

Your email address and the display name you choose. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us. We never see your Google password, and your Hottest Gist password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form that no one can read.

What you do on the site

Your likes, reactions, comments and replies, poll and Top 10 votes, quiz results, fan wall messages, daily check-ins, saved posts, follows, friends and friend requests, private messages you send to friends, and reports you send.

What you choose to share

  • Fan profile and posts: your name, bio, profile picture, posts, photos and videos.
  • Services board: your listing and the contact details you put on it.
  • Song submissions: artist and song details, your story, links, cover art and the WhatsApp number or email you give us.
  • Newsletter: your email, only if you sign up.
  • Phone alerts: a technical address from your browser that lets us send notifications, only if you turn alerts on.

Payments

Payments for the gold tick and featured spots are handled by Paystack. We never see or store your card number or mobile money PIN. We keep only the payment reference, amount, currency and date, so we can switch on what you paid for and help with any problem.

Visits

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which stories are popular and where visitors come from. It does not use cookies and does not track individual people.

03How we use it

  • To run your account and show your name next to what you post.
  • To show likes, comments, votes, the Top 10, Top fans and your friends.
  • To send you the notifications and alerts you asked for.
  • To review song submissions and contact you about them.
  • To process payments and switch on paid features.
  • To keep the community safe: filtering spam and abuse and acting on reports.
  • To understand which stories people read, so we can write more of what you enjoy.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

04What other people can see

Some things are public by design: your display name, profile picture, bio, fan posts, comments, wall messages, Services listings and follower and friend counts. Stories and comments can be read by visitors without an account. Your email address, phone number on submissions, payments, votes, friend requests and private messages are not shown to other users.

Private messages

Messages between friends can only be seen by the two people in the chat. Our team does not read them, except where needed to investigate a serious report of abuse or when the law requires it. Only friends can message each other, so you can stop someone messaging you by removing them as a friend.

06Storage on your device

The site saves a few small things in your browser so it works smoothly: your login session, theme (light or dark), recent searches, posts you liked or saved, and which banners you closed. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. We do not use advertising cookies.

07How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. If you delete content, it is removed from the site. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except payment records, which we may keep for as long as the law requires for accounting.

08Your rights and choices

You can:

  • See what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it: change your name, bio and picture in your profile or settings.
  • Delete your posts at any time, or ask us to delete your whole account.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter and turn off phone alerts at any time.
  • Object to how we use your data, or complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

To make a request, contact us at our WhatsApp channel. We reply within 14 days.

09How we protect it

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) throughout. Access to the database is locked so each person can only change their own content, every action is checked on our servers, and our admin accounts are protected with two-step verification. No system is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and will tell you if a breach affects you.

10Children

Hottest Gist GH is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use the site with a parent or guardian's permission. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. When we do, we change the date at the top of this page, and for important changes we will let you know on the site.

12Contact us

Questions about your privacy or your data? Reach us through our WhatsApp channel.
