Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you sign up we store your email address and the display name you choose. If you use Google sign-in we receive your name and email from Google. We never see your Google password.

When you use the site we store what you create: likes, reactions, comments, wall messages, poll votes, daily check-ins, and (if you choose) your email newsletter sign-up.

How we use it

To run your account, show your name next to your comments, work out the Top fans board, moderate abuse, and, only if you opt in, send you updates. We do not sell your data.

Where it is kept

Accounts and content are stored with Supabase. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may log basic visit data such as page views and country. Embedded players (YouTube, Spotify and similar) are loaded only when you press Play and have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can change your display name or log out in Settings, and unsubscribe at any time. To have your account and content deleted, contact us at the contact details on the site.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this page and will change the date above.
