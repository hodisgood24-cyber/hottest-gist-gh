Nacee's KAVOD 26 brings gospel, theatre and comedy to the National Theatre on November 8

By News desk · 3 November 2026

Plan your Sunday evening, Accra 🎟️

Nacee has announced the second edition of KAVOD, a night that mixes gospel music, theatre and stand-up comedy. It takes place on Sunday, November 8, 2026 at the National Theatre in Accra, starting at 5pm.

On the music side, Nacee will be joined by Soul Winners, Cindy Thompson, Kofi Peprah, Yvonne Mends and Joe Mettle. The theatre line-up includes Vivian Jill, Killer Ntua and Clemento Suarez, while Jacinta, Parrot Mouth and Jeffrey Nortey are among the comedians.

Tickets are GH¢200 for standard and GH¢300 for VIP. For enquiries, call 0597143961.
