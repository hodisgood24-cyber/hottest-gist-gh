Marseille uses Kofi Mole's "Balotelli" in tribute video to Mario Balotelli

By News desk · 15 November 2026

Kofi Mole's song "Balotelli" has got some international love, after French club Olympique de Marseille used it in a video celebrating Mario Balotelli's goals 🎤

The clip put together Balotelli's goals at the Stade Vélodrome. He joined Marseille from OGC Nice in January 2019 and scored eight goals in 15 Ligue 1 games, becoming a fan favourite in his short stay.

The song takes its inspiration from the Italian striker's name.
