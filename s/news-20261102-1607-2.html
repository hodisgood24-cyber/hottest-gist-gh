"Bra" is the longest-running No. 1 on Ghana Apple Music this year

By News desk · 2 November 2026

Kweku Smoke's "Bra" has now spent 36 days at No. 1 on Ghana's Apple Music daily songs chart in 2026, the most of any song this year. "Popstar" is next with 35 days and "Darkest Style" third with 34 days. "Sacrifice" (21 days) and "Don't Come Too Close II" (20 days) complete the top five.

Source: Pulse Ghana
