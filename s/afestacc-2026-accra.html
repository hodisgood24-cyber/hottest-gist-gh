AFESTACC 2026 heads to Accra from December 9 to 12

By News desk · 3 November 2026

Mark your calendar, creatives 🎨

Ghana will host the African Festival of Arts, Culture and Creatives (AFESTACC) 2026 in Accra from December 9 to 12, alongside Industrial Skills Week Africa. Both gatherings are put together by the Ministry of Tourism, Culture and Creative Arts and the African Union Development Agency (AUDA-NEPAD), and they sit on the official "December in GH" calendar.

The arts side is set to include cultural showcases, exhibitions, fashion, food, investment talks and platforms for young people and women running creative businesses. The main programme runs December 9 to 11, and the event wraps up on December 12 with a public wellness walk.

Organisers are inviting creatives, industry leaders, investors and members of the African diaspora. A venue has not been announced yet, so stay tuned.

Source: Pulse Ghana
