Lil Win drops "A Journey to Africa" soundtrack with Medikal and Fameye

By News desk · 10 November 2026

Kumawood actor and filmmaker Lil Win has released the official soundtrack for his upcoming film "A Journey to Africa (Nzulezu)" 🎬 He announced the drop on Facebook on November 9. Medikal delivers the rap verses and Fameye handles the vocals.

The film brings together Ghanaian and Nigerian stars, including Kanayo O. Kanayo, Patience Ozokwor, Mike Ezuruonye, Zubby Michael and Destiny Etiko. It was largely shot at Nzulezu, the stilt settlement in the Western Region, where the community made Lil Win its Nkosuohene.

Fans have been reacting online, with many praising Fameye's vocals and Medikal's creativity.

Source: Pulse Ghana
