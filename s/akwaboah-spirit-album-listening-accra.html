Akwaboah's new album "Spirit" gets its listening event in Accra

By News desk · 13 November 2026

Akwaboah has held the official listening event for his new album "Spirit" in Accra 🎶

A video shared on social media on November 11 shows actress Vivian Jill Lawrence at the event, where she took part in a cake-cutting ceremony and bought copies of the album worth $2,000.

She said Akwaboah's songs have comforted and inspired her, adding that she called his wife at dawn to say how much they mean to her.
