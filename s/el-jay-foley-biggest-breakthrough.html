E.L says Jay Foley gave him his biggest break in music

By News desk · 13 November 2026

Rapper and producer E.L has been telling TV3's Afternoon Show how his music career took off 🎤

He said it began in senior high school with a group called Scallions, who recorded on a small microphone and a Pentium 2 computer. He credits Jay Foley with his biggest breakthrough, saying Foley gave him the chance to go full-time in music after he left his job at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and helped him get early TV exposure.

E.L also said he would not battle M.I Abaga, whom he sees as a senior brother, but would be open to facing South African rapper Nasty C. He added that his song "Koko", about gratitude, was recorded after he moved to the United States.
