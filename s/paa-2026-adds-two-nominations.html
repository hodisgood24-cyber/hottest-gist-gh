Praise Achievement Awards 2026 adds two nominations after petitions

By News desk · 11 November 2026

The organisers of the Praise Achievement Awards 2026 have approved two petitions and added the nominees to the official list 🙏

The new entries are Efua Black's Holy Ghost Concert 2025 in the Christian Music Event of the Year category, and Miss Walters' "Finding Hope" in Urban Gospel Song of the Year.

The petition window ran from October 19 to 23. The announcement did not give a ceremony date.
