GPL matchday 9: Debibi beat Hearts of Oak, Kotoko draw with Medeama

By News desk · 2 November 2026

Debibi United beat Hearts of Oak 1-0 in matchday 9 of the Ghana Premier League, while Asante Kotoko and Medeama drew 1-1. FC Samartex won 1-0 against Bibiani Gold Stars and lead the table on 18 points, ahead of Medeama on goal difference. Hearts of Oak are third on 17 points and Aduana FC fourth on 16 after a 2-0 win over Berekum Chelsea.

Source: Pulse Ghana
