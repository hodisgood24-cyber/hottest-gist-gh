Davido says a Wizkid joint tour would cost $500,000 a show

By News desk · 6 November 2026

Davido has floated the idea of a joint tour with Wizkid, and he says he would ask for $500,000 per show 🔥

Speaking on the IONWANNATALK show, he said such a tour would draw huge crowds around the world. He was less sure about a collaborative single, pointing to the D'banj and 2Baba record as an example of a team-up that did not make much impact.

Davido added that the two have not recorded together in about 10 years, and that he has moved past their earlier disagreements.
