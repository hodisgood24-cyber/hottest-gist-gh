Cartun announces new single "Mula" for November 27

By News desk · 2 November 2026

Ghanaian artiste Cartun is returning after an extended break with a new single called "Mula", out on digital platforms from midnight on November 27. The song blends Afrobeats with urban trap and is about ambition, survival and the daily hustle. Ray Joe produced it, Samsney handled the mixing and mastering, and a visualizer will follow on Cartun's YouTube channel.

Source: Pulse Ghana
