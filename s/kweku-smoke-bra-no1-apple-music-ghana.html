Kweku Smoke's "Bra" takes No. 1 on Apple Music Ghana, and he now holds 13 of the top 20

By News desk · 4 November 2026

Kweku Smoke is having a monster run on the charts 🔥

On the Apple Music Ghana Top 20 for November 2, his song "Bra" jumped from No. 2 to No. 1, pushing "Faith" (with Lasmid) down to second. "Bombele" sits at No. 3 and "Survivor" at No. 5, with Kofi Mole's "BALOTELLI (SUPA)" splitting them at No. 4.

In total Kweku Smoke owns 13 of the 20 places, eight of them in the top 10, and his September album No Days Off is still feeding the chart, with its title track at No. 16.

Elsewhere, Asake and Blaqbonez's "IKEBE 3000" climbed from 12 to 8, and Black Sherif's "Jolie" moved up one spot to 17.

Source: Pulse Ghana
