MOLIY's BADDIES leads the most-streamed Ghanaian projects on YouTube Music in 2026

By News desk · 5 November 2026

Pulse Ghana has published its list of the 10 most-streamed Ghanaian projects on YouTube Music so far this year, and MOLIY's BADDIES sits far out in front with 632.5 million streams 🔥

Black Sherif's Sun Sherif Vol. 1 is second with 85.2 million, followed by KiDi's Where Do We Go From Here at 8.97 million, O'Kenneth's CAME OF AGE at 8.7 million, and the King Promise and Mr Eazi collaboration See What We've Done at 8.2 million.

The rest of the top 10 is Kweku Smoke's No Days Off, Kuami Eugene's Sweet Boy, Samini's Origin8A, Oseikrom Sikanii's 9 and Reggie's Guy Guy Mmienu.

Source: Pulse Ghana
