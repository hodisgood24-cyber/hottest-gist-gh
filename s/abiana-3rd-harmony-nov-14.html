Abiana to release three-track project "3rd Harmony" on November 14

By News desk · 10 November 2026

Singer-songwriter Abiana is dropping a three-track audio and visual project called "3rd Harmony" on Saturday, November 14, on all major streaming platforms 🎶 A pre-save link is already up.

The project is built around love, connection, transformation and permanence, and is split into three movements: NOW, BECOMING and FOREVER. The tracks are "What We Do for Love", "One X One" and "We Will Always Be Together".

He-Brew produced the first two songs, while Nana Kofi (Finurse) produced the third. Abiana describes her sound as "Soul Life", a blend of soul, highlife, neo-soul and contemporary African music.
