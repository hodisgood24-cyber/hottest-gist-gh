M Jay is getting ready to release new single "Ngolo Kante!"

By News desk · 26 November 2026

Ghanaian artiste M Jay has a new single on the way called "Ngolo Kante!", borrowing its name from the French footballer N'Golo Kante ⚽ It is described as a motivational Afrobeat and dancehall tune about perseverance and self-belief.

The news follows M Jay's wins at the Ghana Music Awards Europe 2026, for Best Diaspora Hiplife-Pop Artist of the Year and Best Diaspora New Artiste of the Year.

There is no release date yet, but MyJoyOnline reports that one is expected soon.
