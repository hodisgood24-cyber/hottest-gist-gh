Ghana and Burkina Faso eye new trade deal at SIAO 2026

By News desk · 2 November 2026

Ghana's Trade Minister, Elizabeth Ofosu-Adjare, has called for a new trade agreement with Burkina Faso at the opening of the 18th International Arts and Crafts Fair of Ouagadougou. Ghana is a Guest of Honour at the fair and is showing products from all 16 regions. Trade between the two countries passed US$1 billion last year.

Source: Rainbow Radio Online
