Adom Kiki's Worshipper's Heart concert draws thousands in Kumasi

By News desk · 19 November 2026

Gospel minister Adom Kiki led thousands in worship at the fifth edition of The Worshipper's Heart, held at CCC Tech Atigya in Kumasi 🙏

Esther Smith, Joe Mettle, Kofi Owusu Peprah and Efe Grace ministered as guests, and Nana Ama McBrown was among those in attendance.

There was also a surprise moment when Nana Yaw Ofori Atta, known as One Man Thousand, performed his hit You Dey Feel The Vibe and then joined Kiki to perform a new collaboration live for the first time. The song is expected to be released at a later date.
