I.K. Ansong releases new gospel single "Onyame Asem" with a video

By News desk · 26 November 2026

Gospel musician I.K. Ansong has a new single out called "Onyame Asem", meaning "God's Word" 🙏 The gospel highlife song comes with an official music video and is available on digital platforms.

It draws on John 1:1 and Isaiah 53:5, with a message of faith, healing and hope.
