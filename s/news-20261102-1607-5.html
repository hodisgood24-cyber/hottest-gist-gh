Ghanaian players abroad: Kudus named MOTM, Nuamah scores again

By News desk · 2 November 2026

Mohammed Kudus was named Man of the Match after setting up Archie Gray's opening goal in Tottenham's 2-0 win over Crystal Palace. Ernest Nuamah scored Lyon's opener in a 2-0 win over Angers and has four league goals this season, and Joseph Opoku scored and assisted in Swansea's 4-0 win over Blackburn. Marvin Senaya also returned from injury off the bench for Auxerre, who lost 5-1 to Strasbourg.

Source: Pulse Ghana
