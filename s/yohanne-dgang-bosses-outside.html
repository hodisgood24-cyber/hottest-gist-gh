Yohanne D'Gang returns to Ghana with 'Bosses Outside' for multi-artist album

By News desk · 9 November 2026

Ghanaian recording artiste Yohanne D'Gang is back with a new track called "Bosses Outside" 🎤 It is his contribution to the multi-artist album "Harris Moses Set It", produced by CV Tay and mixed and mastered by ExtremeBeatz.

The album is planned to carry eight to ten songs from artists in Liberia, Ghana and Nigeria, and is executive-produced by Harris Moses Wogbeh, who owns MosMove Entertainment. Yohanne is CEO of Good People Social, the group launched in December 2025.

His sound mixes Afro-fusion, dancehall, Afrobeats and street anthems. The album's release date and other featured artists have not been announced yet.
