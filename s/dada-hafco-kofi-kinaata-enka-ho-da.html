Dada Hafco and Kofi Kinaata team up on highlife single "Enka Ho Da"

By News desk · 15 November 2026

Highlife artiste Dada Hafco has teamed up with Kofi Kinaata on a new single titled "Enka Ho Da" 🎶

The official audio is on Dada Hafco's YouTube channel, runs 3 minutes 13 seconds, and was produced by DDT under Dadabees Empire Ent. A smart link points fans to their favourite streaming platform.

Both artistes were on the Best Highlife Artiste shortlist at the 2021 VGMA, and Kinaata won Songwriter of the Year for a fifth time at the 26th Telecel Ghana Music Awards in May 2025.
