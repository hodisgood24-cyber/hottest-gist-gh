Sarkodie takes his African Diaries tour to Kenya as London O2 show passes 75% sold

By News desk · 7 November 2026

Sarkodie is on the road again 🌍 The rapper has reached Kenya on his "African Diaries" promotional tour, where he met Kenyan rapper Khaligraph Jones and paid a visit to Ghana's High Commissioner, Paul Evans Aidoo.

Tanzania, Ivory Coast and Nigeria are expected to follow, though no dates have been shared yet.

Back home, over 75 percent of tickets for his Rapperholic show at London's O2 Arena in March 2027 have reportedly been sold. The venue holds more than 20,000 people, and the usual Accra Rapperholic is still lined up for December 25.
