Sarkodie drops new single "Nobody" with Rémy Adan ahead of DeJaVu

By News desk · 14 November 2026

Sarkodie has released a new single called "Nobody", featuring Rémy Adan 🎤

The visualizer premiered on Sarkodie's YouTube channel on Thursday, November 12, and the song reached streaming platforms on Friday, November 13. On Apple Music it runs for 3 minutes 28 seconds. The visual was directed by Edward Pappoe and Gus Sarkodee and released through Sarkcess Music.

"Nobody" arrives as a lead-in to Sarkodie's DeJaVu, which he has announced for November 27.
