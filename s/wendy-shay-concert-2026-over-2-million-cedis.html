Wendy Shay says her team spent over GH¢2 million on Shay Concert 2026

By News desk · 28 November 2026

Putting on a big free show doesn't come cheap. In an interview with Nana Romeo, Wendy Shay said her team spent more than GH¢2 million on this year's Shay Concert.

The fifth edition was held on November 21 at the forecourt of West Hills Mall in Weija, Accra, with free entry. She said the money went into things like the stage, sound, lighting, security, water, a health screening and gifts for bloggers and the media.

Sarkodie made a surprise appearance and Black Sherif joined in on stage. Other performers included Kofi Kinaata, Fameye, Lasmid, Obrafour, Sista Afia and Kofi Mole 🔥
