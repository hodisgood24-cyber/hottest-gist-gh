Okyeame Quophi says Highlife helped revive Becca's career with "Hw3"

By News desk · 13 November 2026

Media personality and former Akyeame member Okyeame Quophi says he played a part in reviving Becca's career with the song "Hw3" 🎺

In a video shared online, he said Becca was struggling to reach audiences across Africa and had gone without a hit for a while. He said his wife, Stacy, introduced Becca to him, and he suggested she reconnect with Ghanaian listeners through Highlife.

According to him, the song became a hit about a week after release. "Ghanaians love Highlife," he said.
