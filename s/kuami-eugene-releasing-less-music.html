Kuami Eugene says he is deliberately releasing less music

By News desk · 7 November 2026

Kuami Eugene is slowing down on purpose 🎤 In an interview on Pluzz FM, the singer said he wants to see whether fans keep supporting him through longer gaps between releases.

He says royalties, publishing and investments now bring in steady income, so money is no longer his main driver, and he hopes to reach a level like Shatta Wale's where "you don't have to do much".

He plans to give his single "Stranger" more time before the next project, and says he is not leaving music.
