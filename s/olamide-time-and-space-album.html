Olamide drops 10-track album "Time and Space"

By News desk · 18 November 2026

Olamide has released his 13th studio album, Time and Space, which landed on streaming platforms on Monday, November 16 🎧

The project has 10 tracks and runs about 33 minutes. FOLA is the only guest, joining him on "Jaiye", while Olamide carries the rest of the songs alone. The tracklist includes "Elohim", "Skoskola", "Say Less", "Burukutu", "Alujon" and "Jogor".

It comes through his YBNL Nation label with EMPIRE. "Burukutu" was the earlier single, out since October 20, with a video directed by TG Omori.
