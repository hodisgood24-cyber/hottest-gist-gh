GoldBod projects $1.2 billion in FX for November

By News desk · 2 November 2026

The Ghana Gold Board expects its small-scale gold trade to bring in about $1.2 billion in foreign exchange in November 2026, and plans to release $800 million of it to commercial banks. In October it generated $1.011 billion, all sold to banks through its GoFX platform. The board says it will keep supporting the build-up of Ghana's foreign reserves with the Bank of Ghana.

Source: Rainbow Radio Online
