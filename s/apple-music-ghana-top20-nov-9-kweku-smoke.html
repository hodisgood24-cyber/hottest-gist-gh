Kweku Smoke keeps No. 1 on Apple Music Ghana as he holds 11 of the top 20

By News desk · 9 November 2026

Kweku Smoke is still running the Apple Music Ghana chart 📈 "Bra" stays at No. 1 this week, with "Faith" featuring Lasmid at No. 2, "Bombele" at No. 3 and "Survivor" at No. 5. In all, the rapper holds 11 of the 20 spots.

Kofi Mole's "BALOTELLI (SUPA)" sits at No. 4. Asake has five songs on the list, led by "Forgiveness", which climbs five places to No. 6, while "B4 B4" by Davido, FOLA and Mayorkun jumps four places to No. 10.

Two songs are new this week: Blaqbonez and Asake's "Chanel" at No. 17 and Asake's "WHY LOVE" at No. 20. Fameye's "Gen Z" rises to No. 18 and Black Sherif's "Jolie" slips to No. 19.

Source: Pulse Ghana
