Sarkodie calls on government to put more funding into Ghana's film industry

By News desk · 19 November 2026

Rapper Sarkodie has urged the Ghanaian government to back the film industry with more funding 🎬

In a post on X on November 19, he compared Ghana's storytelling potential with the way Hollywood has shaped America's image worldwide, saying Africa has not yet fully used creative content to change perceptions.

He argued that film can boost tourism, attract investment and strengthen national identity, and said the creative sector is often left out when budgets are approved. His message: "Let's revive our film industry."

Source: Pulse Ghana
