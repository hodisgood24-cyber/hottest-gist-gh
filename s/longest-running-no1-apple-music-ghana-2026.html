"Bra" is now the longest-running No. 1 on Apple Music Ghana this year, at 36 days

By News desk · 4 November 2026

Numbers don't lie 📊

A Pulse Ghana tally of Apple Music Ghana's 2026 chart shows Kweku Smoke's "Bra" has spent 36 days at No. 1, the most of any song this year. "Popstar" is right behind on 35 days and "Darkest Style" has 34.

Next come "Sacrifice" (21 days), "Don't Come Too Close II" (20) and "Biggest Nathaniel" (18). "Faith" has held the top spot for 12 days, and "Bombele" for 10.

Sixteen different songs have reached No. 1 so far in 2026, so the race for the top is busy.

Source: Pulse Ghana
