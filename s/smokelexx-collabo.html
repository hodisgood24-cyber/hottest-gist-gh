Meet Smokelexx: the Rulers Records star getting ready to drop "Collabo"

By Hottest Gist GH · 2 November 2026

Keep your eyes on Smokelexx 🔥

Born Emmanuel Nartey, the rising Afrobeats singer and songwriter is signed to Rulers Records, and he has built his name the hard way: performing on high school stages across Ghana and winning over crowds one campus at a time. That grind carried him into the mainstream with his debut single, and now he is ready for his next move.

Up next is "Collabo", produced by BeatMasterNanaKay. The artwork sets the mood: Smokelexx sitting close with a lady in a sunflower field, drawn in warm cartoon style. It looks like a sweet, laid-back love song, the kind that lives on your soft-life playlist.

The teaser is out now on TikTok, so get your speakers ready 🔊 Tap Play below to watch it right here.
