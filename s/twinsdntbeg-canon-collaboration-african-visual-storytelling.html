TwinsDntBeg team up with Canon to back young African creatives

By News desk · 23 November 2026

Ghanaian photography and videography duo TwinsDntBeg have announced a collaboration with Canon 📸 The twin brothers, who work under Swag of Africa Studios, say the partnership is about supporting emerging African photographers, filmmakers and content creators.

They are also looking at a masterclass covering how to plan a shoot, cover live events and turn photos and footage into stories.

The duo's music-video work includes the visual for Rocky Dawuni's "Rise", which earned a Music Film of the Year nomination at the Black Stars International Film Festival in 2025.
