20 shows lined up in Ghana from November 20 to New Year's Eve

By News desk · 17 November 2026

December in Ghana is shaping up to be busy 🎟️ Pulse Ghana has pulled together a list of 20 shows running from November 20 to December 31.

KiDi's Crazyworld is on December 4 at GHUD Park, Lasmid performs on December 5 at the Palms Convention Centre, and Gyakie plays GHUD Park on December 12. Black Sherif's Zaama Disco is on December 21 at the University of Ghana Sports Stadium, with Stonebwoy's Bhim Concert following there on December 24.

Sarkodie's Rapperholic is listed for December 25, Victony and Young Jonn play Future Club on December 28 and 29, and King Promise's 5 Star Show is on December 30 at GHUD Park. Prices range from free entry to GH₵2,000 for premium access, and some venues are still to be announced.

Source: Pulse Ghana
