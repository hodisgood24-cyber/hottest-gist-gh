Phlakes releases street-inspired single "Outside" ahead of DLAR Vol. 2

By News desk · 16 November 2026

Phlakes is back with a new single called "Outside" 🎤

The street-inspired record has a catchy hook built around the North Side and South Side, and speaks about different lifestyles and experiences. It is available on Audiomack.

"Outside" is a preview of his upcoming tape, Dreamers, Leaders and Rebels Volume 2, which focuses on identity, growth and new perspectives.
