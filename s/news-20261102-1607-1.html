Kweku Smoke's "Bra" takes No. 1 on Apple Music Ghana chart

By News desk · 2 November 2026

Kweku Smoke's "Bra" has moved up to No. 1 on the Apple Music Top 20 Ghana for November 2, 2026, replacing "Faith", which slips to No. 2. Kweku Smoke also holds No. 3 with "Bombele" and No. 5 with "Survivor", while Kofi Mole's "BALOTELLI (SUPA)" sits at No. 4. Asake, Black Sherif and Fameye also have songs in the top 20.

Source: Pulse Ghana
